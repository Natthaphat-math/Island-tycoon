Day Theme Lab

Three takes on the island's daytime music. They share one tune and chord progression, so you're comparing sound and groove. Play one, switch the time of day, and tap the coin pads along with it.

Time of day

morning midday afternoon

Morning is sparse: marimba or soft lead, light shaker, no kick. Midday brings in the full band. Afternoon drops some drums and gets warmer and softer, heading into dusk. Layers fade over a couple of seconds instead of cutting.

Tap along

–streak
Snap to beat
Taps within 2.6s of each other make a streak. Each step climbs the scale, and from 3 in a row an extra percussion layer joins in.

What to listen for

  • After a few loops: does it still feel good, or start to nag? You'll hear the day music for hours.
  • Energy: upbeat enough to keep you tapping, without competing with the game?
  • Coins: do tuned, beat-snapped taps feel like playing along? Turn tuning and snapping off to compare. The 16th snap adds up to about 0.13s of delay.
  • Phone speakers: try it on your phone too. Bass mostly disappears there, so the tune and marimba carry it.
